P14 · the recommendation, and why

Reco holds no spend of its own. It projects three streams — ledger.expense.* into a spend profile, portfolio.card.* into the cards you hold, and the compacted catalog topic into card terms and reward rules — and scores every published card against the profile: 60 % on the rupees it would save you, 40 % on how well its rewards sit where you actually spend. Add an expense below and the ranking moves. A card that would cost more than it earns goes in the not recommended bucket however good its fit. Testing aid only — deleted in P19.

1 · Spend something POST /v1/expenses (ledger)

This writes to the ledger. Live: the event travels through the outbox, the relay and Kafka, and a sweeper recomputes the account about three seconds after it goes quiet — so the page polls rather than claiming the ranking has already moved. 

—

2 · The spend profile the scorer's only view of you

Monthly averages per category root over the 3-month window. A rolling window cannot be kept by deltas alone, so the deltas land in a per-month table and the windows are rebuilt from it.

3 · Recommended GET /v1/recommendations

Not recommended below the threshold, or it loses money

4 · Why? GET /v1/recommendations/explain/{cardProductId}

Pick a card above to see the arithmetic: what each category root earns, which caps bound it, the fee after its waiver probability, and the milestone.

5 · Cards you hold projected from portfolio

Add a card on p11-portfolio.html and it appears here through portfolio.card.added — a held card is marked in the ranking rather than hidden from it, because "you already have the best card for this" is an answer worth seeing.
