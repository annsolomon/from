P13 · budgets, liabilities and what-if scenarios

A budget has a period (weekly, monthly, or one custom window), an optional category subtree and a limit. Spend is never entered here: the ledger publishes ledger.expense.created and planning's consumer applies a delta to every budget the expense matches, in the period its Asia/Kolkata day falls in — so add an expense below and watch the bar move. Each alert threshold fires at most once per period (planning.budget.threshold-crossed). A liability's schedule comes from the shared EMI engine, the same one behind /v1/calc/emi. A scenario is a copy of the account taken when it was saved, so evaluating it tomorrow still answers the question it was saved to ask. Testing aid only — deleted in P19.

1 · Budgets POST /v1/budgets

Thresholds default to 50, 80 and 100 %. A monthly budget repeats on its startsOn day of the month, clamped in a short one.

2 · Spend something POST /v1/expenses (ledger)

This writes to the ledger, not to planning. Live: the row goes out through the outbox, the relay and Kafka, so the bars catch up a moment later — the page polls /v1/budgets/status for a few seconds rather than claiming they already have.
