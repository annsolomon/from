P17 · commerce: deals that rank, links that are signed, money that is counted once

The marketplace ranks deals by 0.8·(0.6·card-match + 0.4·savings) + 0.2·sponsor - card-match is a ranking branch, never a filter, so a deal for a card you do not hold is still listed, lower. A deal's saving is every component on the full spend, each capped on its own (₹75,000 at 10 % cap ₹5,000 + 5 % cap ₹2,000 = ₹7,000). Every click goes through a signed, expiring /r/ link: one raw row, one Redis increment, a 302 with the click id as subid; the counters are flushed every 10 s. Affiliate postbacks are HMAC-signed and idempotent by (network, ref). One open card application per card; an operator decides. Ads rotate by weight, stop at each visitor's frequency cap, and PREMIUM is ad-free. Testing aid only - deleted in P19.
